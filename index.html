New to Wrestling? Start Here.

You do not need any experience to join. Plenty of our wrestlers walked into the room having never wrestled a match — here is how you get on the mat.

1. Get cleared

Complete a current sports physical, register through the Mountain Crest athletic office and finish the required online forms. Nobody practices until paperwork is cleared.

2. Show up

Bring athletic clothes, wrestling shoes if you have them (we can help if you don't), a water bottle and a hair tie. Everything else we teach you.

3. Get connected

Download sportsYou and join the team channel. That's where practice changes, weigh-in times and bus schedules get posted.

Girls wrestling is the fastest-growing high school sport in the country, and Utah sanctions a full girls state championship. There is a weight class for every athlete — you compete against wrestlers your own size.

Grit, Grace & Gratitude

Three words carry this program:

  • Grit. We do hard things. It's okay to lose. It's not okay to quit.
  • Grace. Composure and character in every win and every loss, and kindness to everyone — teammates, opponents, officials and fans.
  • Gratitude. We thank the people who make this possible, and we appreciate the chance to compete.

We care most about the two things every athlete controls: attitude and effort. A hard-fought loss can be a great day; a lazy win isn't. And the season is built to peak in February, so early results are not the measure of how we are doing.

Grades come first. Athletes must stay eligible under UHSAA and Cache County School District academic rules to compete.
