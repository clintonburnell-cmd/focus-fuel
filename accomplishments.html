Accomplishments

Five 4A team state titles in the first six seasons of sanctioned girls wrestling in Utah — the year-by-year record book.

Year by Year at State

Every Mountain Crest girls wrestler on record as placing at the UHSAA state championships — champions and podium finishers alike, season by season. Utah sanctioned girls wrestling with its own state tournament beginning in 2020–21, and Mountain Crest won the 4A team title that first year.

Help us finish the record book. Every wrestler who placed at state belongs on this page — champions, runners-up and everyone else who made the podium. What is here was compiled from published coverage of each state tournament, which named the champions and some of the finalists but not the full top-six brackets, so there are still gaps: the medal counts in each season header show how many names are missing. If you placed at state, or your daughter did, send it to the coaching staff through sportsYou and it goes on the board. Spot something wrong? Tell us and we will fix it.

Program Milestones

Team

  • 4A state team champions: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026
  • Four straight titles, 2021 through 2024
  • Champions in the first sanctioned season — 427 points to runner-up Bear River's 171 in February 2021
  • Eight individual champions in one tournament (2022) — the program's single-season high
  • 2025: runner-up to Bear River, the only season the Mustangs have not won 4A
