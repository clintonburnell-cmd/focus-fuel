Titan Mindset Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Titan Mindset is a daily motivation app for wrestlers. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your reminder settings: whether reminders are on, your morning and evening times, and the optional push server address.
  • A record of which day's reminders have already been shown, so you are not notified twice.
  • A cached copy of the app so it opens offline.

This information stays in your browser or app storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

What the app collects

Nothing. Titan Mindset has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app asks your device for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device using your device's clock and time zone.

Optional push server

If you choose to connect a push server under Reminders → Advanced, the app sends that server a push subscription token issued by your browser, your time zone name (for example "America/Denver"), and your chosen reminder times, so that server can deliver reminders when the app is closed. No name, email, or other personal information is sent. The server operator is whoever runs the address you entered; when you clear the address the app unsubscribes.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app publisher through the Google Play listing.
