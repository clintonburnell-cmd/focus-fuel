Contact Us

The team runs on the sportsYou app — here is how to get in, and how to reach the coaching staff.

We communicate through sportsYou

Everything day-to-day goes through the team's sportsYou channel — practice changes, bus departure times, weigh-ins, results, photos and last-minute updates. If it affects your wrestler this week, that is where it gets posted.

Every wrestler and at least one parent or guardian should be in the channel. This website carries the season schedule, the record book and how the program works; sportsYou carries everything that changes.

How to get access

The channel is invite-only, so send Coach Burnell an e-mail and he will add you:

E-mail clint.burnell@ccsdut.org

To save a round trip, include:

  • Your wrestler's full name and grade
  • Your name and how you are related to her
  • The e-mail address and mobile number you want on the team channel

Once you are added, download the free sportsYou app from your phone's app store and sign in with that e-mail address.

New to the team and not sure where to start? Nothing to sign up for here — see the FAQ for what it takes to join, and Pre & Post Season for the 6:00 am class that is open to anyone who wants to try wrestling.
