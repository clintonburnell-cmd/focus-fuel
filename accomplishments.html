Accomplishments

Five 4A team state titles in the first six seasons of sanctioned girls wrestling in Utah — the year-by-year state and divisional record book.

Year by Year at State

Every Mountain Crest girls wrestler on record as placing at the UHSAA state championships — champions and podium finishers alike, season by season. Utah sanctioned girls wrestling with its own state tournament beginning in 2020–21, and Mountain Crest won the 4A team title that first year.

Every Mustang who has ever placed at state is on this page — all 98 of them, champions and sixth-place finishers alike, with weight class, finish and season record, straight from the official state brackets. Every season since the sport was sanctioned is complete. Spot an error? Tell the coaching staff through sportsYou and we will fix it.

Divisional Results

Divisionals are the state-qualifying meet — 4A splits into two divisions and the top finishers advance to state. The Mustangs have won their division each of the last three seasons.

Program Milestones

Team

  • 4A state team champions: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026
  • Four straight titles, 2021 through 2024
  • Champions in the first sanctioned season — 427 points to runner-up Bear River's 171 in February 2021
  • Eight individual champions in one tournament (2022) — the program's single-season high
  • 2025: runner-up to Bear River, the only season the Mustangs have not won 4A
