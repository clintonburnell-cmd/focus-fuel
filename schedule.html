Team Schedule

Duals, tournaments and team events. Times are subject to change — confirm in sportsYou before you drive.

Add to your calendar (.ics)

Heads up: weigh-in times are typically one to two hours before the first whistle and are posted in sportsYou for each event. Wrestlers ride the team bus to away events unless a parent has filed a transportation release with the athletic office.

Practice

Monday through Saturday in the wrestling room, starting Monday, Nov 9. Practices run about an hour and three quarters plus strength and conditioning through November and December, then get shorter as we shift to conditioning and individual work in January and February.

Days off: Christmas week (Dec 21–26), MLK Day (Jan 18) and Presidents' Day (Feb 15).

Home mat

Mountain Crest High School, 255 South 800 East, Hyrum, UT. Home duals are in the main gym; home tournaments run in both gyms.

Two big family dates

The auction on Nov 18 is our main fundraiser, and we host the MC Filly Invitational on Jan 2 — a full day that needs table workers, scorers and concessions help. Sign up in sportsYou.
